About the playoff model

The model forecasts each team's remaining scoring and plays out the rest of the regular-season schedule 12,000 times. The displayed probabilities are the share of simulations in which each team qualifies, wins its division or earns the #1 seed.

What predicts scoring?

Current-season Potential PPG, the GM's prior All-Play percentage in Weeks 9–17, and prior Potential PPG in Weeks 12–14 estimate scoring ability. Weights are learned from earlier seasons at the same observed week. Team estimates are centered on league YTD PPG. Prior history follows the GM; a new manager does not inherit the previous occupant's performance.

Historical FAFL scoring determines weekly variation and uncertainty about each team's ability. Forecast-week bye adjustments use the selected roster and eligible same-roster replacements. Weekly variation and strength uncertainty are fitted around these adjusted scoring estimates. Simulations use a fitted Normal-inverse-Gaussian distribution and retain one uncertain ability draw per team across the remaining weeks.

H2H, All-Play, all five Bonus Games and playoff qualification share the same simulated points. Potential Points use a random nonnegative gap conditioned on scoring, observed team gaps and NFL bye counts.

Historical testing

The expanded study tests 2023–2025 and official 2026 results through Week 4, using only earlier seasons for fitting. Scoring, matchup, All-Play and Bonus probability errors are evaluated together. Completed seasons also test playoff qualification. The selected model is F315 without an extra team-spread growth factor.

Qualification and seeding

Each conference sends four division winners and three wildcards. Division ties use overall winning percentage, head-to-head among the tied teams, All-Play, points and Potential Points. Wildcards use winning percentage, All-Play, points and Potential Points. Once selected, playoff teams are seeded by All-Play, points and Potential Points, without a division-winner preference.

The model includes the four three-week bonus games and the season-long bonus game. Forecasts cover qualification and opening playoff seeds, not the subsequent championship bracket. The Consolation Ladder lists the teams outside the current field.

Reading the report

Hover, focus or tap PPG, projected wins and playoff odds for the breakdowns. Colors indicate league rank. Current seeds are based on completed results; projected seeds apply the rules to the expected final standings. A projected seed is not a team's average simulated seed.

Status letters reflect the simulations: b means #1 seed in every trial, d means division winner in every trial, p means playoffs in every trial and e means no playoff appearances. Whole-percent rounding can show 0% or 100% without one of those labels.

Updates and limitations

Refreshes are scheduled Tuesday at 1 a.m. Eastern for unofficial results and Thursday at 5 a.m. Eastern for official results. Hosting schedules can run late. The report shows the actual refresh timestamp, and failed data checks preserve the last published report.

Historical data retains each season's original scoring rules; it has not been rescored under the 2026 rules. Current 2026 results come directly from MFL. Late-season activity can affect the prior-year inputs. The model does not separately forecast future injuries or roster moves, and it does not model shared-player scoring dependence across conferences.
