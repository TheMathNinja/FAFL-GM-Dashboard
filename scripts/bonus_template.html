About the model

Cutoffs are the mean simulated midpoint APW between ranks 15/16 for a win and 17/18 for a tie. Points For breaks APW ties, followed by Potential Points.

Simulated Potential Points are at least as high as Points For. The gap varies with scoring, each team’s observed gap, and the number of NFL teams on bye.
